# What Would Win — narrative robustness 0.8.2

**Identity:** application 0.8.2 · model 0.5.0 · data 0.5.0 · share v5 · storyboard v3 · custom/history v3

## Outcome

Application 0.8.2 makes the short account explain the winner through a concrete, evidence-backed route. It remains downstream of the immutable result and does not change coefficients, trials, roster data, winner probabilities or draw handling.

## Causal claim rules

- Reasons are netted and selected at the precise narrative concept before they are deduplicated for display. Mass, scaling and defensive quality can no longer license one another's prose.
- Quantity records expose declared counts separately from their pressure basis. A shared continuous log-pressure threshold decides whether reserve depth is present across storyboard construction, validation, evidence and reader copy; rounded display counts never decide the branch.
- A fully active pair is described as two simultaneous threats. The account never says “only about two,” “fresh attackers” or “reserves” for that state.
- Larger ordinary groups may show an approximate attack-pressure equivalent, but the prose explicitly says this is not a literal count of bodies attacking at once. Conceptual populations use capped aggregate pressure and never fall through to small-group or same-exchange wording.
- The opening includes the selected first exchange; the turning point uses the selected causal transition instead of repeating a reason card.
- Contact modes and defensive morphology may provide profile-backed texture only when a supported close-contact route exists. They cannot claim an individual wound, body-part injury or exact strike unless a structured applied effect records it.
- Every visible profile fact, factor, ability, quantity and verdict remains linked to an evidence record in the current storyboard.

## Example boundary

For one velociraptor against two North American porcupines, the model supports two-on-one simultaneous pressure and stronger defensive resistance. The profile also supports saying that closing brings the velociraptor into quill-contact range. It does not record a specific puncture, so the story must not say that the velociraptor was impaled, punctured or spiked.

## Validation

The regression suite covers the exact shared matchup, requires reserve language only for a positive continuous pressure gap, tests a gap that rounded counts would erase, blocks literal small-group wording at conceptual scale, forbids tautological active-count wording, preserves seeded determinism and result immutability, and sends every built-in profile through the layperson pipeline in both roles.

The same release also keeps plural contestant pronouns grammatical and makes the optional single-creature size reference participate in the responsive card grid, preventing the selector from overlapping the card edge at narrow desktop widths.

Model/data remain 0.5.0, share stays v5, and storyboard remains v3 because this release changes only evidence construction, causal selection and reader wording.
