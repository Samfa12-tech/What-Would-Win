# What Would Win — narrative clarity 0.8.1

**Identity:** application 0.8.1 · model 0.5.0 · data 0.5.0 · share v5 · storyboard v3 · custom/history v3

## Outcome

Application 0.8.1 replaces the technical story shown to casual readers with a short battle retelling and direct reasons why the winner wins. It does not change the numerical engine, coefficients, trial count, roster, scenario, winner, probability or draw boundary.

## Reader contract

- Simple and Deep-dive Story views share one `LaymanBattleStory`.
- The account has exactly three stages: opening, turning point and finish.
- The story is 45–150 words and each visible sentence is at most 32 words.
- Both contestants act in the opening; the turning point states what changes; the finish names the outcome.
- One to three reason cards explain only mechanism families with a positive net contribution for the authoritative winner.
- Every stage, reason and alternate path retains evidence that exists in the current storyboard.
- Raw factor IDs, log power, coordination exponents, frontage terminology and other model language stay out of the casual account.
- The opposing side's best supported path remains visible in Deep-dive Story mode.
- Draws do not enter the story builder and never receive an invented winner sequence.

## Causal repair

Reason ranking now nets winner-benefiting and loser-benefiting factors within the same reader mechanism family before a claim is eligible. Generic contact attacks are not presented as disabling special abilities. Quantity-one fights never use crowd logic, physical comparisons cannot claim a smaller contestant is larger, and the finish always names the winner. Close results use uncertain language, while one-way route decisions are identified as route decisions rather than 100% trial win rates.

The model result remains immutable throughout story construction. Story seeds may alter presentation variants only; they cannot change causal selection, the winner or the probability.

## Presentation

Simple mode now leads with the winner and model percentage, one to three short reasons, and the three-part battle story. Its notice says plainly that the story is not a replay of an individual trial. The previous technical verdict, raw key factors and Monte Carlo language are removed. Deep-dive Story mode uses the same account with evidence markers anchored to their final words, the other side's best chance and a quantity explanation. Analyst mode and the complete seven-phase reconstruction remain available on demand.

## Validation scope

- Representative semantic fixtures cover resized animals, group pressure, flight, ranged and area attacks, gaze conditions, restraint and fixed hazards.
- Every built-in profile enters the layperson pipeline in both matchup roles; decisive results must pass the story contract.
- Unit tests preserve deterministic output, exercise close and one-way results, force the safe layman fallback, protect custom names, and verify that story construction cannot mutate the authoritative result.
- Browser checks cover Simple and Deep-dive Story views, desktop/mobile reflow, keyboard behaviour, evidence controls and serious automated accessibility findings.
- Fresh visual evidence is retained under ignored `output/narrative-audit/`; it is QA evidence, not a deployable asset.

Physical-device and manual screen-reader validation remain outside the automated evidence boundary.
